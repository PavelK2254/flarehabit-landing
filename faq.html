FlareHabit FAQ: the Android goal planner

FlareHabit is a goal planner for Android. This page answers questions about what it is, how a goal becomes stages, what's free, Premium, and your account. The app is on Google Play. For a short product overview, see the homepage.

On this page: About the product · Pricing and Premium · How it works · Comparison · Privacy and account

About the product

What is FlareHabit?

FlareHabit is a goal planner for Android. You type a goal in your own words, such as run a 5K, and the app splits it into stages with daily and one-time actions. The Goal screen shows finished stages, the stage you're in, and what comes next, shown as Next stage plus its number. The dashboard lists today's actions so you can mark them Done.

Who is FlareHabit for?

FlareHabit is for someone who already knows what they want, but not what to do today. You work on one Goal at a time. The app is Android only for now. An iOS version is planned, with no release date. It isn't built as a points-based habit tracker.

Is FlareHabit available on iPhone?

Not right now. FlareHabit is Android only, and it needs Android 7.0 or newer. It's on Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.flarehabit.app. An iOS version is planned, with no release date.

What language is FlareHabit in?

The app is in English only.

Pricing and Premium

Is FlareHabit free?

You can install the app from Google Play and use Stages 1 and 2 of a goal without paying. You type a goal, see it split into stages, and use the dashboard during those first two stages. The app has no ads. Later stages need a Premium subscription. There is no separate free trial, because Stages 1 and 2 are the trial.

What does Premium include, and what does it cost?

Stage 3 and later stages of a goal require Premium. There is one subscription: €39.99 a year, which is the default, or €9.99 a month. The amount is shown in the app before you buy, and it may vary by country.

Is there a free trial or a lifetime plan? How do I cancel?

There is no free trial and no lifetime plan. Stages 1 and 2 are the trial. Premium renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancel in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting your account or uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

How it works

How does FlareHabit turn a goal into stages and today's actions?

You type a goal in your own words and get a set of stages. On the Goal screen you can see which stages are done and which one you're in, and what comes next. Each stage can have daily and one-time actions. You complete a stage once its requirements are met, then you move on. The dashboard lists today's actions. You review the suggestions and decide what to keep.

Can I change the suggested stages or actions?

During stage setup you can approve or skip a suggested action or habit. You can also delete a habit later from its details screen. A deleted habit stays in the stage as incomplete. You cannot add new actions, rewrite their text, or reorder them.

Do I work on more than one goal at a time?

You work on one Goal at a time. That is a limit in the app, not just a recommendation. Every action belongs to a stage, and every stage belongs to that goal. You can start a new goal when you are ready. If you still have an active goal, starting a new one abandons the current one.

Does FlareHabit send reminders?

Yes. You can pick a time for one daily Today's focus reminder. The reminder is a local notification, scheduled on your device.

Does FlareHabit work offline?

You can open the app, see today's actions, and mark progress without a connection. Creating a new goal or continuing to the next stage needs a network connection. Progress is saved on the device and syncs when you are signed in and back online.

Comparison

Is FlareHabit a habit tracker?

No. FlareHabit is a goal planner. Some older descriptions still call it a habit tracker. A habit tracker is built around repeating checkboxes. FlareHabit starts from a goal you type, splits it into stages, and shows today's actions for that goal. Daily and one-time actions can sit inside a stage, but progress is measured by the goal and its stages, not by points.

Is FlareHabit a good goal planner app for Android?

FlareHabit is a goal planner for Android, and it's on Google Play. You type a goal in your own words, get stages, and see today's actions on the dashboard. You work on one Goal at a time. Stages 1 and 2 are free, and later stages need a Premium subscription.

Does FlareHabit have achievements or streaks?

Yes. Next to your goal plan, the app has achievements, and your profile shows your best streak. They're extras. The core is the plan itself: your goal, its stages, and what to do today.

Privacy and account

What data does FlareHabit collect? Are there ads?

There are no ads. You need a Google account to create goals and sync your progress. Payments go through Google Play, and the developer does not receive or store your card details. The app sends data over an encrypted connection, and you can request that it be deleted. For the full list of what is collected and which services are used, see the Privacy Policy at https://pavelk2254.github.io/flarehabit-docs/Privacy-Policy.html.

How do I delete my FlareHabit account?

You can delete your account in the app under Settings > Delete account. If you no longer have the app, follow the instructions on the delete-account page at https://pavelk2254.github.io/flarehabit-docs/delete-account.html: email pavel4567@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "FlareHabit account deletion request". Deletion from active systems is within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel Premium. Cancel the subscription in Google Play if you also want to stop renewal.
